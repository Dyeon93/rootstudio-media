rootstudio-dm

rootstudio-dm is a server-side assistant operated by Rootlab Inc. for its own Instagram professional account, @therootstudio_official. It has no separate website or login screen; people use it through Instagram.

What it does

  1. Publishes our Reels and carousel posts to @therootstudio_official on a weekly schedule, with Instagram's AI-generated label.
  2. When someone comments on one of our posts to request the free guide, it replies to the comment and sends one private reply asking them to follow us and reply "완료" (done).
  3. After they reply, it checks that they follow our account and sends one message with a link to the free guide (a public Notion page).

How to use it

Open instagram.com/therootstudio_official, choose a post, and leave a comment.
